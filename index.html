四足机器人Quadruped Robot

基于 STM32 的四足机器人系统，完成了基本的行走、转向和避障功能，支持远程控制。STM32-based quadruped robot system, completed basic walking, turning and obstacle avoidance functions, supporting remote control.

STM32 无刷电机+IMU融合BLDC + IMU Fusion RS-485 SBUS 嘉立创 EDAJLC EDA

充分调研了当前市场上的四足机器人设计及设计需求，电机采用宇树 GO-M8010-6 永磁同步电机，绘制分电板，用来设计整体硬件架构。主控使用嘉立创 EDA 基于 STM32 设计 RS-485、CAN、UART、IIC 总线的四层 PCB 板，完成了电路设计、PCB 绘制、焊接软硬件调试等工作，最终实现了四足机器人系统，完成了基本的行走、转向和避障功能，支持远程控制。After thorough research on current quadruped robot designs and requirements, the robot uses Unitree GO-M8010-6 permanent magnet synchronous motors. A power distribution board was designed for the overall hardware architecture. The main controller is a 4-layer PCB based on STM32 designed with JLC EDA, integrating RS-485, CAN, UART, and IIC buses. Work included circuit design, PCB layout, soldering, and software/hardware debugging. The final system achieves basic walking, turning, and obstacle avoidance with remote control.

关键技术Key technologies：4 层 PCB 设计，运动学正解，运动学逆解，IMU，SBUS 通信协议4-layer PCB design, forward kinematics, inverse kinematics, IMU, SBUS communication protocol

超级电容Supercapacitor UID Keil5 SolidWorks

设计太阳能电动车，参与 2025 中国大学生工程实践与创新能力大赛"重走长征路"主题赛道。车辆需自主循迹，精准抵达瑞金、延安等 13 个历史节点（初赛 10 固定点，决赛 13 随机点），融合红色文化与绿色科技，考验工程全链路能力。设计过程中，负责电路设计与调试，采用太阳能板供电，设计高效能量管理系统，确保续航能力。通过优化电机控制算法和车体结构，实现精准循迹和稳定行驶。最终成功完成比赛任务，获得优异成绩。Designed a solar-powered electric vehicle for the "Retracing the Long March" track of the 2025 China College Students Engineering Practice and Innovation Competition. The vehicle must autonomously track a path and accurately reach 13 historical nodes such as Ruijin and Yan'an (10 fixed points in the preliminary round, 13 random points in the final), blending revolutionary culture with green technology and testing full-cycle engineering capability. Responsible for circuit design and debugging: powered by solar panels with an efficient energy management system to ensure endurance. By optimizing the motor control algorithm and vehicle structure, precise tracking and stable driving were achieved. The competition task was completed with excellent results.
